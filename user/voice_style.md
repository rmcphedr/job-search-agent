# Voice & Writing Style

For future resume tailoring and cover letters (not used in MVP evaluation skills).

## Tone

- Professional, concise, evidence-led
- Lead with impact and methods, not buzzwords
- Scientific credibility without excessive jargon

## Structure preferences

- Bullet points with strong verbs
- Quantify where possible
- Tie skills to domain outcomes (healthcare, neuroscience, ML)

## Avoid

- Generic AI hype without substance
- Overclaiming seniority beyond postdoc → industry scientist transition
