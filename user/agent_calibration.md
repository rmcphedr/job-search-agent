### 2026-08-05 — Industry, experience, location, and production-readiness calibration

- Fully prioritize industry roles. Short-term academic postdoctoral roles are not targets, regardless of technical fit.
- An industry postdoc is viable only when it is an exceptional fit and pays at least CAD 85,000.
- Count relevant PhD and postdoctoral research generously toward requested years of experience; do not treat all academic years as zero industry-equivalent experience.
- Toronto and Waterloo are acceptable but should receive a moderate location penalty relative to Montreal and Vancouver. A sufficiently strong opportunity can outweigh that penalty.
- Score production-oriented AI roles partly for strategic growth potential. The candidate expects to have a production-ready system and potentially a paying client by the end of August 2026.
- Until that system/client evidence exists, distinguish near-term production readiness from confirmed production deployment; do not invent cloud, uptime, monitoring, scale, or client-delivery claims.

### 2026-08-05 — Domain specificity versus consulting breadth

- Narrow or specialist ML/data-science roles require strong alignment with biomedical engineering, neuroscience, healthcare, biotech, or adjacent scientific domain expertise.
- Banking and insurance are low-motivation sectors. A pure ML scientist role in those sectors should score below threshold unless it offers an unusually compelling bridge to the preferred domains or agentic business-intelligence direction.
- Broad consulting roles are an exception to the domain-alignment requirement because cross-domain problem solving, client variety, and translating ambiguous needs into systems are intrinsically valuable.
- Consulting roles involving agentic engineering, business intelligence, rapid prototyping, and stakeholder-facing implementation should receive meaningful positive weight.
- LangChain and similar tools may be presented as usable near-term capabilities because they can be implemented quickly in existing projects. Do not claim lengthy production tenure or client impact until that evidence exists.

### 2026-08-05 — Employer setting, secondary work, and compensation context

- Hospital and research-institute staff roles are acceptable. Do not penalize them merely for being outside a commercial company; assess permanence, seniority, compensation, and trajectory separately.
- Well-paid part-time expert or consulting work is attractive as a stop-gap or secondary opportunity, although full-time industry employment remains the primary target.
- Broad consulting does not erase all domain preferences. Accounting and assurance are lower-motivation application areas, so an otherwise strong agentic consulting role in those areas should receive a moderate sector penalty.
- Compensation is contextual, not a hard filter. Moving costs and local cost of living must be justified by higher pay than the approximately CAD 80,000 Montreal baseline.
- Working expectations: approximately CAD 100,000+ for Toronto, around CAD 90,000 for Vancouver, and roughly CAD 80,000 minimum for compatible US-remote work. Consider role quality, growth, benefits, permanence, and relocation costs rather than applying these as absolute cutoffs.
- The realistic target compensation range is CAD 100,000–120,000.
- Innodata's health-AI evaluation role is confirmed as a very strong target. The Amaris pharmaceutical bioinformatics role was independently flagged by the user, confirming its high fit.

### 2026-08-05 — International visibility, frontier stretches, wet lab, and business-facing work

- Keep strong non-Canadian in-person roles visible. Do not exclude them automatically, but require a higher overall fit because relocation, immigration, and work authorization add friction.
- Frontier biomedical-ML roles such as Valence Labs are acknowledged long shots. Keep them visible as aspirational opportunities, but distinguish domain excitement from current qualification readiness.
- Wet-lab-only roles are low priority. Experimental biology is valuable supporting evidence, but target roles should retain meaningful computational, data, ML, agentic, or business-intelligence content.
- Business-facing work is appealing when it still uses technical judgment: client discovery, requirements translation, data/project leadership, workflow redesign, decision support, and communication between scientific, technical, and business stakeholders are positive signals.
